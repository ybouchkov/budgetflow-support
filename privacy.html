Who is responsible

BudgetFlow is provided by Yani Buchkov, Sofia, Bulgaria, who is responsible for the personal-data processing described in this notice. For privacy questions or requests, contact ybuchkov@gmail.com.

What BudgetFlow processes

BudgetFlow processes the information you enter: income, expenses, categories, notes, savings, investments, payday settings, credit limits, card-payoff records and transfers between tracked balances.

Sign in with Apple supplies an app-specific account identifier and, when provided by Apple, your email address, which may be an Apple private-relay address. BudgetFlow does not receive your Apple password.

If you enable Wallet capture through Apple Shortcuts, the automation supplies payment details such as the amount, currency, merchant, date and card label. This is optional and does not give BudgetFlow unrestricted access to your Wallet, bank account or payment history. BudgetFlow does not request full payment-card numbers or card security codes.

Local preferences include notification schedules, tutorial progress, balance-visibility settings, a salted PIN verifier and a dated record of the privacy-notice version you acknowledge. iOS handles Face ID and Touch ID; BudgetFlow receives an authentication result, not your face or fingerprint data.

Why we use it

We use budget and account information to provide the features you request: calculating balances, recording and reviewing transactions, tracking savings and credit, and restoring and syncing your budget. Our legal basis for these necessary service functions is performance of the contract for providing BudgetFlow, under GDPR Article 6(1)(b).

Local security controls support our legitimate interest in preventing unauthorized access, under Article 6(1)(f). We process information needed to meet applicable legal duties, including data-protection requests, under Article 6(1)(c). Support correspondence is used to answer your request and resolve the issue.

Optional Wallet capture and reminders remain under your control. Where a separate processing activity requires consent, we will request it separately and provide a way to withdraw it. Acknowledging this notice is not blanket consent for advertising, tracking or unrelated processing.

Storage and services

The release app stores budget records on your device and synchronizes them with the private database in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. Sync needs an available iCloud account and connectivity. Development builds use a separate local store.

The Apple account identifier, email, PIN verifier, privacy acknowledgement and local settings are stored in the app's local preferences. BudgetFlow does not put the PIN verifier or privacy acknowledgement in its CloudKit budget database. Local app data may be included in device backups according to your Apple and iOS backup settings.

Apple provides authentication, iCloud and the App Store under its applicable service and privacy terms. If you email us, your message is handled by our email provider and any other service you choose to send it through. We may share information where required by law or necessary to handle a legal claim.

The current app does not include third-party advertising or behavioral-analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal data or use your budget for advertising. Apple may separately process diagnostics or App Store information according to your Apple settings and its terms.

International processing

Apple and email-service providers may process information outside Bulgaria or the European Economic Area. Their applicable privacy notices describe their processing locations and transfer arrangements. We do not promise that all information remains in Bulgaria or the EU. Contact us for information about relevant safeguards and how to obtain a copy. Apple's privacy policy is linked below.

Retention and deletion

Budget records are kept to provide your ongoing budget and history until you delete them. More → Delete All Budget Data removes the app's financial records; deletion synchronizes to iCloud when connected. This resets your budget, keeps your sign-in and security preferences, and does not delete your Apple Account. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete the iCloud budget.

Local security preferences and privacy acknowledgements remain in the app's local data until that data is removed. Backup copies follow the retention and deletion behavior of your backup provider. Disable the Shortcuts automation if you want future payment capture to stop.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to answer your request, resolve outstanding issues, meet applicable legal duties or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The reason for retaining it and the relevant obligations determine its retention period. Contact us about account-related deletion or any information you cannot remove yourself.

Your choices and rights

You can edit budget records in the app, delete budget data in More, change notification preferences, and disable Wallet capture in Shortcuts. Providing the account and budget information needed for a feature is necessary for that feature to work; optional features need not be enabled.

Depending on the legal basis and circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability of data you provided, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Email ybuchkov@gmail.com to make a request. We may need proportionate identity verification and will normally respond within one month; any permitted extension will be explained within that period.

You can complain to a supervisory authority, including Bulgaria's Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP), or the authority where you live or work. The CPDP complaints page is linked below.

Security, decisions and changes

BudgetFlow uses iOS authentication and local security controls, with a salted PIN verifier rather than readable PIN digits. Its calculations and summaries help you understand your own entries; it does not make credit, lending or other decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

We will update this notice when practices change. You can always read the current version in More → Privacy Policy. A changed notice version may require a new acknowledgement. This acknowledgement records that you have read and accepted the notice; it does not remove your data-protection rights.
