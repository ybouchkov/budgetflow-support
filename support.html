Common questions

How do Wallet payments reach BudgetFlow?

Wallet capture requires an optional automation in Apple Shortcuts. Start with More → Payments → Wallet payments and follow the setup. BudgetFlow cannot silently create or monitor every Apple Pay transaction. Online Apple Pay checkouts are not covered by the documented card-tap trigger.

Why do Savings totals differ from Edit Month?

The Balance Card shows accumulated savings and investments. Edit Month shows only what you added for the selected month. Transfers from savings increase Available and reduce accumulated savings.

What if Face ID or Touch ID fails?

If you have set up a PIN, choose Use PIN at login or in the biometric fallback. Existing users can set up or change a PIN in More → Account. The same PIN can unlock sensitive balances.

How do I delete my budget?

More → Delete All Budget Data removes financial records. Cloud deletion synchronizes when connected. Signing out or uninstalling alone does not delete your iCloud budget. The financial reset keeps your sign-in and local security settings and does not delete your Apple Account.

How do I make a privacy or account-related request?

Email ybuchkov@gmail.com. See the privacy notice for rights, retention and storage information. Budget reset removes financial records; it is separate from deleting your Apple Account.

How do I delete my BudgetFlow account?

In version 3.0 build 35 or later, open More → Account → Delete BudgetFlow Account. Confirm with your PIN or device authentication. Keep the app connected to the same iCloud account until deletion completes; if interrupted, reopen the app and retry. This removes your budget and this device’s saved sign-in and account settings. Development builds delete local test data only.

After deletion, disconnect BudgetFlow under Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → BudgetFlow → Delete or Stop Using, or use account.apple.com. This does not delete your Apple Account. Disable BudgetFlow Shortcuts automations and remove local settings on other devices too. Delete All Budget Data remains a separate budget reset that keeps sign-in and security settings.
